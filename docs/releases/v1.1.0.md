## New: Vault for passwords and 2FA

Passwords and two-factor codes, right in the Notch. Everything is encrypted on your PC and never goes anywhere else.

- **Passwords** with name and username or email. Copy either one with a click, show the password, edit it, or create a strong one with the built-in generator.
- **2FA codes** (TOTP) with a live countdown. Add one by taking a screenshot of the QR code (Win + Shift + S) and pressing **Ctrl + V**, or type the key by hand. Then give it a name.
- **4-digit PIN** before a password or code is shown or copied. You can turn it off for passwords or for 2FA separately in **Settings → Tools → Vault**; turning it off needs the PIN. After five wrong tries the vault locks for 30 seconds, and the lock doubles from there.
- **Kept out of clipboard history.** Copied passwords and codes don't show up in the Notch clipboard or in Windows' Win + V history, and they are cleared from the clipboard after 30 seconds.
- **Local only.** The vault is encrypted with Windows DPAPI and bound to your Windows account. 2FA keys never leave the app's backend; only the six-digit codes reach the interface.

The vault is off by default. Turn it on under **Settings → Tools**.
