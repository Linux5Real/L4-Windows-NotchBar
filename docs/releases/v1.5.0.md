## New: volume per app, right in the player

<p>
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Linux5Real/L4-Windows-NotchBar/v1.5.0/docs/media/player-mixer.png" width="70%" alt="The player with the per-app volume mixer open: Spotify on top, then System, Google Chrome, Discord and a muted Steam" />
</p>

- **Mixer like in Windows.** The arrow next to the playback controls opens a list of every app that plays sound, plus the system volume. Drag a bar or scroll on it to change the level (hold Shift for bigger steps), click an app's icon to mute it. The app that's playing sits on top; when another app takes over, the rows slide into place. ([#4](https://github.com/Linux5Real/L4-Windows-NotchBar/issues/4))
- **Jump to the playing app.** Click the cover or the title to bring Spotify, Spotifast, your browser or whatever is playing to the front. For browsers, the window showing the track wins. An app closed to the tray (like Spotify) opens its window again. ([#5](https://github.com/Linux5Real/L4-Windows-NotchBar/issues/5))

## Also new

- **°C or °F, 24 h or 12 h.** Pick the temperature unit under **Settings → General → Weather** and the clock format under **Settings → General**. On first start both follow your Windows region. With °F, wind is shown in mph.
- **Presentation mode.** Hides clipboard previews, portfolio amounts and the vault while you share or record your screen. Timer, music and the mic/camera dots stay visible. Switch it with the eye in the Overview, or let it turn on by itself while a screen recording is detected (Teams, Discord, OBS and others that use Windows screen capture). Settings live under **Settings → Display**.
- **Focus mode as a thin line.** Focus mode can now shrink the notch to a thin line at the screen edge instead of making it half transparent. Music and other activities are hidden then; click the line three times to get the notch back. Pick the look under **Settings → Display → Focus look**. ([#7](https://github.com/Linux5Real/L4-Windows-NotchBar/issues/7))
- **Focus mode in the tray menu.** Right-click the tray icon to turn focus mode on or off. The check mark always matches the notch, however you switched it.
- **What's new, inside the app.** **Settings → General → Updates** lists what changed in each version. After an update, a dot on the gear shows what's new since the version you had before, including versions you skipped. Before you install an update, you see what it brings.

## Fixes

- **Clicks right below the notch work again.** Pressing the mouse anywhere near the notch made its invisible drop zone grow, so clicks on tabs or toolbars just below it were swallowed. The zone now only grows while you actually drag something towards the notch. ([#7](https://github.com/Linux5Real/L4-Windows-NotchBar/issues/7))
- **Much lower memory use with images in the clipboard history.** Copied images and screenshots are now kept compressed instead of as raw pixels. A 4K screenshot takes about 2.5 MB instead of 31 MB, so a history full of screenshots no longer adds hundreds of MB.

## Polish

- **Smooth cover changes.** When the song or the app changes, the new cover fades in over the old one, in the closed notch and in the player. No more hard swap or flash of the note icon.
- **Calmer player.** Next track, play and previous sit centered; volume lives in the mixer instead of an extra slider.
